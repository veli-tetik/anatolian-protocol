Method

  • Scope: the program company only. It sells the program, employs the care team and pays partners; it owns no clinic.
  • Timing: each program runs 12 months, half recognised in the enrolment year, half in the next.
  • Demand: qualified leads × 10% conversion, plus referral clients and companions. 5% of sign-ups cancel before the first visit; 3% withdraw mid-program with a pro-rata refund.
  • Markets and months: 4 markets by year four, each with entry and running costs; the first 36 months are also modelled monthly.
  • Cash: 70% paid at enrolment, the rest at month six; 65% of partner costs paid in the enrolment year.
  • Currency and tax: USD excl. VAT and flights; USD/TRY 49.5; 25% tax after loss carry-forward; no grants.

Year one, and what drives the need

Year one: where the money goes

  • The monthly view goes deeper: cash bottoms out in month 18 at $0.79M, $145k below the year-end figure.
